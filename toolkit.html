    Good to know
    
The different ways you'll use Claude

    
It's all Claude — you just meet it in different places depending on the job.

    

      
Claude Chat
The everyday assistant you talk to. Ask a question, get a draft, think something out loud.
Quick help & drafting

      
Claude Skills
Saved "recipes" that make Claude do one job your exact way, every time — you just call it by name.
Repeatable tasks, done consistently

      
Claude Cowork
Claude that does the multi-step work, connected to your tools — where your newsletter gets built & scheduled and your client list stays updated. Needs a paid Claude plan (about $20/mo); everyday Chat is free.
Getting real jobs done

      
Claude Code
Claude that changes the website itself (it's built from code) — a new service, new hours, a new photo. Darice usually drives this one.
Website updates

    

    
In short: you'll mostly live in Chat and Cowork; Darice handles Code; and Skills make the things you do often as easy as calling them by name.

    

      
ChatDraft a message, reply to a review, or word something tricky.

      
CoworkSend a promo or newsletter, update your client list, check your calendar.

      
Darice · CodeChange the website — a new service, new hours, a new photo.

    

    
How to start: open the Claude app, tap New chat, and type it in plain words — exactly like the examples above. Your newsletter skill (coming once you're set up) makes that one a single ask.
